Hi, I'm Nathan Nadler-Nir
a designer, filmmaker, and creative technologist building digital gardens. Based in Cape Town, South Africa.

I work across media, from video, illustration and 3D to UX/UI and Web. Whether I'm shaping a brand, producing a documentary, or sketching out a product, I always put people at the center of the process.

Over the years, I have:
  • Produced mountains of video content with companies, collaborators & arts festivals
  • Led the creative strategy of South African tech startups like Momint and Dado
  • Developed custom web experiences for international clients
  • Specialized in creative execution, story structure, and productivity systems

My practice balances creative problem solving with the joy of experimentation, it's grounded in collaboration, fuelled by curiosity, and informed by a lifelong obsession with digital tools.
